\section{Results and Findings}

\subsection{Construct Usage}

The final corpus contains 402 repositories, obtained from 5,009 candidate repositories identified through GitHub's dependency-graph dependents. The static analysis identified several distinct FastAPI dependency-injection constructs.

Depends() was the most prevalent construct, occurring in 303 repositories (75.4\%), with 25,166 call sites and a median of 22 calls among repositories using the construct. Annotated[..., Depends] occurred in 104 repositories (25.9\%), comprising 5,913 call sites. Generator/yield-based dependencies were observed in 162 repositories (40.3\%).

Security dependencies were observed in 42 repositories (10.4\%), with 163 Security() call sites.

\subsection{Depends() Argument Shapes}

Across the 25,166 Depends() call sites, bare function references accounted for 24,444 calls (97.1\%) and occurred across 297 repositories. Placeholder forms accounted for 429 calls (1.7\%), while module aliases accounted for 266 calls (1.1\%). Title-case classes and class methods accounted for 21 and 6 calls, respectively.

\subsection{Dependency Graph Structure}

Non-empty dependency graphs were extracted from 294 of the 402 repositories. These graphs contained 16,494 function nodes, 728 class nodes, and 23,223 directed edges. Class nodes occurred in 126 repositories.

A total of 227 nodes had at least 10 dependents. The highest observed fan-in counts were associated with get\_db (4,683 dependents), get\_session (1,732), get\_current\_user (1,471), and require\_admin (1,175).

Chained dependency relationships were observed, with a median maximum dependency depth of 2 and a maximum observed depth of 7.

After removal of resolver-generated placeholder nodes, graph sizes had a median of 19.5 nodes (IQR: 8--54), with a maximum of 1,444 nodes. The median unresolved-reference rate was 0\% (IQR: 0--3.4\%), although one repository had a 100\% unresolved rate.

Overall, 180 of the 294 graph-bearing repositories (61.2\%) had no unresolved dependency references. Conversely, 114 repositories contained at least one unresolved dependency reference. A further 108 repositories (26.9\% of the complete corpus) had empty dependency graphs despite satisfying the repository-level eligibility criteria.

\subsection{Annotated Types and DI Idioms}

The most frequently observed types in Annotated[...] dependency declarations included User (749 occurrences), AsyncSession (616), str (458), and Session (355). Other frequently observed types included Principal, UserPrincipal, CurrentAdminUser, AuditSink, AuthIdentity, Settings, and Any.

Several additional DI idioms were detected, although most occurred at low frequency. Classmethod-based dependencies were observed in one repository across six call sites, while constructor-injected classes occurred in seven repositories across 14 distinct classes. A provide\_* namespace pattern occurred in one repository, and current\_* request-scope injection occurred in 15 repositories. Module-alias dependencies occurred in 11 repositories. No repositories contained an observed *Repository-typed injection pattern.

\subsection{Data Quality Issue (Resolved Now)}

The resolver generated file:line-style placeholder nodes when a Depends() call occurred outside an identifiable function context. Such nodes occurred in 122 of 294 graph-bearing repositories and accounted for 713 of 17,222 raw nodes (4.1\%). These nodes were removed before computing the cleaned graph-size statistics.
